Privacy Policy

Columba is a decentralized communication application.

Data Collection: None. Columba does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, telemetry, or usage statistics.

Data Storage: All user data, including keys and messages, is stored locally on the device.

Encryption: All communications are end-to-end encrypted via the Reticulum network stack.

There is no central authority; therefore, there is no one to collect your data.
